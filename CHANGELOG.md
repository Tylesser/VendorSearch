# Changelog

## 1.5.0

- **Mounts and pets** get the green **Known** mark too when you already have them, and the **Unknown recipes** check box is now **Unknown**: it shows recipes you haven't learned and mounts and pets you don't have yet.
- **Can afford** check box: shows only what you can buy right now (the gold, and the items or currency an item costs).
- **Buy several stacks at once:** Alt-click a stackable item sold for gold, type how many (or click Max), and Buy makes one purchase per stack, as many as your gold, the vendor's stock and your bags allow.

## 1.4.0

- **In your language**: German, French, Spanish, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese game clients now show the addon in their language (other clients stay in English). Russian clients get the game's own font for headings, since the addon's fonts have no Cyrillic letters.

## 1.3.0

- **Unknown recipes** check box under the search box: shows only recipes you haven't learned. Recipes you already know get a small "Known" mark.
- **Usable** check box: shows only items your character can use.
- Both combine with the search words, work in Fade mode too, and reset when you close the vendor.
- **In bags**: each vendor item shows how many you already have (bags and bank), as a backpack icon and a number. On by default; untick it to hide.
- Entry in the addon menu by the minimap (Retail and WoW Forever) and a `/vsearch help` command.

## 1.2.1

- The feedback window (`/vsearch feedback`) also points to tylesser.com for the other Tylesser addons.

## 1.2.0

- A `/vsearch feedback` command: it shows the link to tylesser.com/feedback, where you can report a bug or suggest an idea.

## 1.1.0

- New Tylesser look for the search box, the Fade check box and chat messages, shared by all Tylesser addons.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Search box on the vendor window that filters the vendor's item grid by item name, type, subtype or equipment slot.
- Paging follows the matching items; matching items keep all the normal vendor actions (right-click to buy, tooltips, linking, preview).
- Match count and "no matches" message; search clears when the vendor closes.
- Fade option: fade non-matching items on the vendor's normal pages instead of hiding them, with the pages that contain matches listed.
- Alt-drag to move the search box, `/vsearch reset` to put it back.
