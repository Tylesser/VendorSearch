# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Search panel next to the vendor window: search by item name, type, subtype or equipment slot.
- All vendor items in one scrolling list with quality colours, stack size, stock and price.
- Click to show the item in the vendor window, right-click to buy, Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click to preview.
- Sort by name or price; filters for usable and affordable items.
